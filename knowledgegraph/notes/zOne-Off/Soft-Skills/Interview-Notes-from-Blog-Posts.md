# Alisa Liu
## Tips
 - Don't use AI coding tools
 - Read interview descriptions and prepare for that specifically
 - Use other companies as practice interviews
 - Collaborate and be social
 - Reconnect with people from along time ago
 - Technical Skills and knowledge matter more than research experience
 - Studying carries a lot of side benefits in terms of creativity, etc.



## Interview Types
1. ML Coding - PyTorch >> NumPy
2. General Coding (Leetcode)
3. Technical Discussion, depth and breadth, parallelism, GRPO vs DPO, positional info
4. Research Discussion
5. Behavioral Interview
    1. AI safety/societal impacts
    2. Make sure to have anecdotes
6. Math - probability, linear algebra, calculus
7. Job Talk

# Yong Zheng-Xin
1. Only 1-2 papers matter during job search
    1. You can always pivot to a new field without being established there.
    2. But that also means the past success is not weighted that much.
2. How can you solve the team's problems on the spot?
3. Interviews have wildcards, e.g.
    1. Asyncio
    2. How to use coding agents
4. Work trials - can last up to a week
5. Timing matters a lot
6. A lot of interviews are not related to your topic, some are broader

# Nathan Lambert
1. Senior employees
**2. If you are junior, you must be obsessed: "since ChatGPT came out I’ve been fully obsessed with LLMs"**
3. No reason to do a phd if you have an offer to do modeling research in a frontier lab, and you don't wanna be a professor.
4. Avoid all negative signals - e.g. a junior researcher being a middle author on too many papers.
**5. You can tell if someone is a genius by reading one tweet from them**
  1. An AI slop blog post will kill your application


# Silvia Sapora
1. 3+ first author papers and one internship/industry role for callbacks
    1. More papers do not help beyond this point
2. Ask yourself questions while preparing for technical interviews - they will often ask the same questions
3. Ask an LLM to create practice questions, there is often a lot of overlap
4. Leetcode
    1. Breadth matters more than depth
    2. Blind 75 and neetcode 150
    3. Don't spend too much time on hards
    4. 150 mediums total
    5. 20 minutes or less per medium
    6. Tensor puzzles
5. Baseline:
    1. Implement a transformer end to end
    2. Implement causal, cross, and self attention
    3. Flash attention
    4. Attention backward pass
    5. MLP forward and backward
    6. Training loop in pytorch or JAX
6. Be proactive, actually reach out to someone
7. There is a list of topics at the bottom of this blog post
